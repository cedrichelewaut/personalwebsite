#### In September 2025, I rejoined Proximus's international carrier business BICS (a Proximus Global company) to head the GTM of their fraud prevention and security solutions. The aim? To get commercial exposure and gain technical expertise on security solutions at an international scale.   
  
##### How BICS does GTM  
  
In my day-to-day, I build out the GTM playbooks for our security products. At BICS we center GTM around four key pillars: the value proposition, the target customer, sales enablement and marketing.  
  
  
 It's a role which offers a unique opportunity to  develop more technical depth and gain real commercial exposure, something which is often lacking in people coming from a more technical background.  
  
I am actively engaged with industry associations such as the GSMA to bring security and innovation to the telecommunications sector. In June I got to get on stage to talk about how visible security can improve protection.  
  
#### Responsibilities  
  
Since September 2025 I have been responsible for a set of +- 10 products related to either fraud prevention or network security. Since August 2026, I have taken up additional responsibilities for solutions made by 365squared (A Proximus Global company), BICS' positioning on AI and soon I'm taking up authentication solutions as well.
