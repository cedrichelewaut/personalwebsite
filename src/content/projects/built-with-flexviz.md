Earlier this year, I got together with Jeroen, a friend who - together with his brother - has developed many great solutions for data analytics on large scale data sets. The latest result being FlexViz.tech. 

FlexViz is unique in that it is the most effective way to visualize large data sets and one of the best ways of analysing data with agents, as the single state in which FlexViz stores a chart view allows agents to easily gain insights in what you are looking at at a specific point in time. 


Together we have looked at how to productise FlexViz and decided the best use of the solution is to open-source it. Since, adoption has been growing steadily among both people who have known Jeroen's other libraries and new people.


Personally, I have used FlexViz to explore large data sets related to books. More on this following soon.
