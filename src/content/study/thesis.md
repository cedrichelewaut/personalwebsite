In my master's dissertation, I explored how Machine Learning can connect immersion in multimedia content to physiological signals, primarily electroencephalogram (EEG) data.

#### Main Contributions

**Objective condition classification:** EEG data can effectively distinguish between users watching content in objectively different settings.

**Subjective rating classification:** EEG data can accurately predict when a user will rate an experience as immersive.

#### Implications

The thesis shows that cognitive processes such as immersion can be modeled with EEG. These findings could enable personalized immersive experiences in emerging multimedia formats, with implications for entertainment, healthcare, and education.
