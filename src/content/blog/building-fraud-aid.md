Fraud Aid supports people in Belgium who have been scammed in getting their money back, either by doing it themselves or with the help of an expert.

[![The plan Fraud Aid gives after a few questions](/img/fraud-aid-demo.webp)](/fraud-aid-demo/)

**[Try the demo](/fraud-aid-demo/)** (it runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere; please do not enter real personal data). The [code is on GitHub](https://github.com/cedrichelewaut/fraud-aid).

## Two ways to get help

- **Do it yourself, free.** A short questionnaire gives a five-step plan: call Card Stop, report to the police, call the bank and send a written claim, claim the money back, protect yourself. It includes a claim letter and a police report in English, French or Dutch, and a first estimate of the case: strong, possible, unlikely or unclear.
- **Ask an expert.** An expert handles the claim for a success fee of 20% plus VAT, 10% more if the case is escalated, and nothing if nothing is recovered.

The estimate and the documents come from fixed rules, not from AI. AI only rewrites the victim's own words into a short statement, which they check.

## Status

This is a prototype and is not yet safe for real cases. It still needs hosting and security for personal data, and review by a Belgian lawyer and a fraud specialist.
