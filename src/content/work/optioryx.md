#### Student Employee at early start-up

In the final year of my master's, I joined Optioryx as a job student. This start-up specializes in AI-powered packing solutions that help logistics companies reduce transportation costs and achieve sustainability goals.

When I joined, Optioryx only had two employees, Bart & Gilles. Since then, the company has grown to become a staple in the Ghent start-up scene. Be sure to visit their website at [optioryx.com](https://optioryx.com) to see the amazing work they're doing.  


#### My Contribution

During my time at the company, I focused on optimizing their 3D bin-packing algorithm. I explored edge cases where the algorithm underperformed and proposed a new data structure called Open Spaces to improve its efficiency. As far as I know, Optioryx still uses this data structure today.
