#### Making an Impact

In 2024, I attended a meeting of Belgium's Engineering Without Borders initiative. I was inspired by how the organization brings together a community of engineers working on projects that create lasting impact on the world. I decided to become a member.

Since 2025, I have supported a clean water project in Somaliland. This year our project lead went on a mission to Somaliland, together with several other organisations, among which MO, who wrote the following Dutch article about the situation in the country and the impact of climate change: [MO.be](https://www.mo.be/reportage/De-klimaatopwarming-is-voor-ons-geen-item-in-het-journaal-het-is-ons-leven)


Since 2026, I am part of the board & organisation of IZG.

#### Join Us

If you're an engineer looking to make a positive impact, feel free to reach out to me or explore IZG on our website: [izg.be](https://izg.be).
