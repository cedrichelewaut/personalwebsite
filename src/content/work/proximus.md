In September 2023, after completing my engineering degree, I joined Proximus as a technical graduate to complement my technical background with broader business experience. Three rotations through product management and strategy reshaped the way I work.

#### Fraud & Security Product Management at BICS

My first rotation was in product management at BICS, Proximus's international carrier service. For eight months, I worked across the product lifecycle, from exploring opportunities for innovative products to tracking development and building partnerships. I gained hands-on experience in market research, business cases and technical reviews.

#### AI Strategy at Proximus Ada

At Proximus Ada, Proximus's AI division, I worked on a broad series of strategic projects instead of owning products, from AI productization exercises to researching agentic AI applications for the telecom industry.

#### Streamlining Operations

In my final rotation, in the operational excellence strategy team, I optimized a network product line, from market research and analyzing the operational model to financial modeling of costs. I learned to collect data across the organization and combine it into strategic insights that prioritize improvements.

#### What I Learned

The graduate program gave me a broad perspective with deep dives in critical areas. I learned to manage complex projects without clear directions or straightforward solutions, and grew from a computer science engineer into a professional at the intersection of technology and real-world impact.
