After my bachelor, I wanted to go further in computer science. Following my first internship at In The Pocket, where I got a first taste of machine learning in practice, I started the Master of Computer Science Engineering at Ghent University. I took courses on machine learning, security, distributed systems and more, building the technical foundation I still rely on today.

However, the master was about much more than courses. A lot of what shaped me happened next to the lecture halls, and each of those experiences has its own post on this site.

#### First master year

In my first year I took an elective on sustainable computer architecture, which let me follow a passion of mine: how the way we design hardware and software determines the energy it consumes, and what we can do to make computing more sustainable.

I was also selected for the Innovation for Society excellence program, where I worked with engineers from other disciplines on a case about racial bias in facial recognition.

#### Second master year

In my second year I went on exchange to Polytechnique Montréal and worked as a job student at the logistics start-up Optioryx, where I improved their bin-packing algorithms.

I closed the degree with my thesis, which focused on modelling immersion with physiological data. After finalizing it, I graduated cum laude.
